## Unit header

**Unit purpose (as-is).** `src/views/auth/` is the client-side body of every `/auth/*` page: login (password + magic-link OTP modes), invite-only signup notice, OTP-first password recovery, recovery-completion (`ResetPassword`) and invite-acceptance (`SetPassword`) password forms. It also holds the three pieces those pages share: the two-column branded shell (`AuthLayout`), the live password-strength meter (`PasswordStrengthMeter`), and the post-auth role-based redirect hook (`useRoleRedirect`).

**Module-level observations (cross-file facts inside the unit).**
